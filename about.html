About the project

A literature-based resource for research questions and subsequent results in inventory management.

Purpose and scope

The catalogue brings together conjectures, questions, and extensions explicitly stated in source papers, alongside later results. Each record preserves its model, benchmark, and scope. Related papers provide context; their inclusion does not establish that they resolve the question.

The current catalogue contains eight source-stated questions or extensions and five additional result records. One further status update is incorporated into its original question, rather than counted twice. A pending review is not a certification that a problem remains open, and a reported resolution is not an independent proof audit.

Contributions and corrections

To suggest an addition or correction, provide the paper, a stable link, the relevant page or theorem, and a concise description of the question or subsequent result. Contributors will be acknowledged with their agreement.
