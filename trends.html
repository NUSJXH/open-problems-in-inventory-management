Bibliometric protocol

Web of Science trend construction

The current charts use 16,819 distinct Web of Science Core Collection records in a reconciled 2000–2026 export completed on 4 September 2026. The six journals are Management Science, Operations Research, Manufacturing & Service Operations Management, Production and Operations Management, INFORMS Journal on Computing, and Journal of Operations Management. The title, abstract, and keyword screen identifies 2,407 inventory-scope records. All document types in the export are retained.

Annual groups use the exported publication-year field, PY. There are 9,897 records in 2000–2020, 5,709 in 2021–2025, and 1,213 in 2026. The 2026 group is incomplete, may include records assigned to later issues, and is neither annualized nor forecast. Charts show 2000–2025 by default.

The archive includes abstracts for 16,297 records and cited-reference blocks for 16,556 records, containing 797,846 reference entries. For 537 distinct records, the exported reference-entry count differs from the reported reference count. These discrepancies remain flagged. Reference availability does not establish complete citation coverage; no citation-network result is claimed here.

Inventory scope requires at least one of the following term families: inventory, stockout, lost sales, replenishment, newsvendor, base stock, order-up-to, or safety stock. Category definitions are applied to titles, abstracts, author keywords, and Keywords Plus. Counts overlap and must not be added. Every open-language candidate is then checked in full text: a keyword match is never sufficient for publication as an open problem.

Chart denominators. In count mode, charts report matching records. In share mode, the overview reports inventory matches divided by all source records in that year; topic panels and heatmap cells report topic matches divided by inventory matches in that year. The six topic panels use one common vertical scale. The table always reports counts. Metadata coverage varies by year; the charts describe the archived sample, not the importance or growth rate of the entire field.

Reproducibility record. The downloadable aggregate contains the exact regular expressions, annual counts and metadata availability, source-subset checksums, journal counts, and cohort comparison. Original-session records take precedence over repeated versions. The union was checked against the live query total and all six journal totals, with per-record ISSN and year checks. No independent accession-number census was exported. Licensed row-level records, abstracts, and reference lists remain in the local archive.

Reconciliation with the earlier snapshot. The former 2021–2025 workbook contains 6,105 records; the new 2021–2025 subset contains 5,709. They share 5,567 accession numbers. Of the 538 records found only in the old five-year cohort, 532 are present in the new archive with publication year 2026 and six are absent from the new archive. The new five-year cohort includes 142 additional records. Fifteen shared records also change year within 2021–2025. These are observed metadata and cohort differences; their underlying causes have not all been established. The earlier aggregate is preserved separately, never spliced into the current series.

Public-data protocol

OpenAlex historical screen

A separate OpenAlex snapshot retrieves 20,854 works assigned to the same six journals through primary_location.source.id, with publication dates in 2000–2026. Of these records, 17,823 have an available abstract. The broad title, abstract, and OpenAlex-keyword screen identifies 2,174 inventory-scope records and 59 records whose abstracts contain open-language phrases. Annual counts are reported separately from the WoS series.

The broad inventory screen additionally includes backorders, spare parts, perishability, shelf life, and deteriorating items. The Lost sales / stockouts category includes both terms. The Data-driven / robust inventory category includes learning, historical or sample-based data, empirical risk, distributionally robust optimization, and robust optimization. The remaining categories screen nonstationarity, censoring, perishability, and supply uncertainty. Exact regular expressions, annual source denominators, and abstract-availability counts are included in the public aggregate data.

These categories overlap. The OpenAlex and WoS screens use different metadata fields and definitions; their levels must not be pooled or treated as interchangeable. Coverage and abstract availability vary by year. The 2026 row is an incomplete indexing-year snapshot and may include papers assigned to later 2026 issues; it is neither a full-year total nor a forecast.

Keyword counts describe the archived metadata, not the importance of a research stream or the number of unsolved problems. Open-language candidates require full-text and subsequent-literature verification before they can enter the paper list.

External registry screen

Comparison with Open Problems in OR

The public Open Problems in OR repository snapshot dated 28 August 2026 contains 159 records, all labeled unverified by that project. The user-specified filter—Applied Probability, Stochastic Processes & Variational Inequalities; Mathematics of Operations Research; solution recorded—returns five problems in variational inequalities, Markov-chain large deviations, monotone inclusions, subgradient derivatives, and KKT stability. None is an inventory-management problem.

A full-repository screen identifies one inventory-adjacent record: Feng, Niazadeh, and Saberi's question about removing a logarithmic loss in Bayesian online assortment with reusable product inventories. The question is not added to the active list because a 2026 working paper by Aminian, Niazadeh, and Nuti states that it solves the earlier reusable-resource problem. It appears instead as a status update, with an explicit boundary between reusable-resource allocation and classical replenishment control.

Supplementary discovery

Beyond the six-journal sample

A separate public-data acquisition contains 14,824 distinct OpenAlex records from completed queries: five supplementary-journal searches, one cross-journal lost-sales search, and four forward-citation searches. The supplementary sources are Mathematics of Operations Research, Naval Research Logistics, European Journal of Operational Research, IISE Transactions, and its predecessor IIE Transactions. This is a search-based supplement, not a full export of these journals.

Broad search can match full text and references. A separate local screen of titles, available abstracts, and OpenAlex keywords retains 6,925 inventory matches, including 1,628 from the supplementary-journal queries. These remain discovery candidates, not verified classifications or new open-problem entries. The source groups overlap and must not be added.

Five cross-journal topic searches and eight further anchor-citation queries remain incomplete because the provider requested a long backoff. One partial topic batch is retained locally but excluded from the complete-query totals. Preprint citation searches and version-aware primary-text review also remain necessary. OpenAlex citation sets are not substituted for WoS citing-article exports. The acquisition summary records query definitions, coverage, and remaining work. Search and citation mechanics follow the OpenAlex search documentation and citation-query documentation.

Acquisition and citation coverage

Separate cohorts, explicit denominators

The September 4, 2026 WoS supplement contains 6,328 distinct records across eight overlapping query sets. Seven observed result sets were acquired in full. The perishable-inventory search remains partial: 2,000 exported rows represent 1,999 distinct records against 3,911 reported results. Completion refers to acquisition of a dated query result, not exhaustive literature coverage.

Of the 6,328 records, 312 also occur in the fixed six-journal archive and 6,016 do not. These discovery cohorts are not pooled with annual trends. Keyword matches require subsequent relevance and model-assumption screening. The archive retains 42 discrepancies between reported and exported reference counts for review.

The eight-anchor citing set contains 142 distinct citing records and 224 exact DOI-matched reference links. Self-citations are retained; one record may cite multiple anchors. The chart reports matches within this archived set, not live citation totals, relative scholarly impact, or resolution of an open question. Version-specific preprint searches, including FP3, remain incomplete.

The aggregate coverage file includes query definitions, observed counts, source-version descriptions, and outstanding evidence. Licensed row-level records and full-text copies are retained locally and are not published. The separate OpenAlex supplement retains its own completion status; WoS acquisition does not retrospectively complete an OpenAlex query.

The new descriptive entry for Xie, Ma, and Xin concerns independent sampled demand trajectories that may contain temporal dependence and nonstationarity. It is not classified as an open question. Best-in-class generalization is distinguished from online censored-demand learning and unrestricted distribution-shift robustness.
